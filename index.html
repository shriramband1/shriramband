Our story

Four decades of leading the way to the mandap

Since 1982 the band has played from East Rohtash Nagar in Shahdara. Families across Delhi know it as “Dilli Ka Mashoor”, and many call us back for the next wedding in the family.

What we play

For every ceremony

From the first step of the baraat to the last dance at the reception.

  • बैंड बाजाBaraat band

    A full band in uniform to lead the procession, with brass and drums that carry the whole street.

  • शहनाईShehnai

    Live shehnai for the entry of the groom and the traditional moments of the ceremony.

  • ढोलDhol

    Dhol players who set the beat and keep the dancing going until the baraat reaches the venue.

  • संगीत व रिसेप्शनSangeet and reception

    Music for the functions around the wedding, planned with the family to suit the evening.

Booking

Three steps to book

Dates fill up in wedding season, so call early.

  1. Call or message

    Share the wedding date, venue and the time the baraat leaves.

  2. Fix the details

    Decide the size of the band and the music you want to hear.

  3. We arrive on time

    The band reaches before the baraat and plays until the groom is at the gate.
